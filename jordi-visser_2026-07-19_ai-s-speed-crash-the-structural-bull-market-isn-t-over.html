Video Snapshot

  1. The AI structural bull market is intact but we are in a 'speed crash' / midcycle digestion phase: the Morgan Stanley tech momentum factor fell ~40% in 18 days, the worst tech unwind on record back to 1998.
  2. Visser frames the new market regime as 'bubbles, parabolas, and speed crashes' driven by AI agents now executing non-emotional, momentum-chasing trades with leverage ('V control') that unwind violently.
  3. He is buying selectively into weakness - added Micron this week, calls 'AI = memory' - but says odds are now 'fair' so he is sitting out the race until better setups.
  4. He expects no massive V-shaped bounce; the correction is a healthy rotation, not a 2007-style systemic event - junk spreads are tight, earnings beats are huge, economy is strong.
  5. His forward beta for the next 12 months is crypto/consumer agents (Ethereum especially), not the infrastructure trade; he sees Bitcoin as 'collateral of the future' and AGI arriving within a few years.
