What was actually said

The Claim

In his weekly video published July 24-26, 2026, Jordi Visser (former Weiss Multi-Strategy chief, now 22V Research principal & the VisserLabs Substack) revealed he had finally published a "Vera Rubin report" — three to four months of work identifying supply-chain names for Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin platform, focused on an earnings "step-up function" in optical and photonics.

“I finally released the Vera Rubin report that I spent, in total, three or four months really working through it, trying to find companies that were not part of the 100-name portfolio. I think there's 25 that are not connected to it. They're still going to be correlated.”— Jordi Visser, weekly video (QHRUwziQ-oE), July 2026
He deliberately does not read the names out on air; the full list goes to paying subscribers (hedge funds, mutual funds).
“The Vera Rubin step-up & the next beta: the 800V DC / Vera Rubin paper (~25 names) targets earnings step-up functions.”— YouTube chapter marker, "AI's Speed Crash Is Creating the Next Opportunity" (vu2zBT3ced8), 37:47
Same report, one week earlier: confirms ~25 names and the 800 VDC power component.
“Visser's broader report identifies roughly 25 names outside his core 100-stock portfolio that will benefit from the Vera Rubin cycle... The Vera Rubin buildout... approximately 25 new names, many Asian, with step-function revenue potential. His 800-volt DC infrastructure thesis also remains in play.”— BigGo Finance recaps of the same episode, July 2026
Independent recap confirms the count, the "outside the 100-name portfolio" filter, and the Asian skew.

Many AsianIndependent recap confirms heavy Asian representation — i.e. the Taiwanese / Chinese / Japanese / Korean optical and power ecosystem, under-covered by US analysts.
Optical / photonics + 800 VDCTwo theses in one paper: Vera Rubin's co-packaged optics (CPO) networking step-up, plus the move to 800-volt DC rack power distribution.

Anchor facts from the video: Nvidia allegedly ramping 1,000 Vera Rubin racks/day = ~$630B/quarter potential; Vera Rubin drives "performance per watt, lowest cost token worldwide"; Marvell remains his largest position and he added AOI the same week.

How to read this

Caveats & Methodology

This is a reconstruction, not the report. The actual 25 names are paywalled (VisserLabs Substack / 22V Research, subscriber side; he posts the research prompts "this weekend" but not the list). Nothing here should be read as Jordi's actual picks or as financial advice.

What is certain: the report exists; ~25 names; outside his 100-name portfolio; "many Asian" (per independent recap); optical/photonics + 800VDC focus; step-up earnings screen; MRVL largest position; AOI added same week.

What is reconstructed: the candidate pool itself — assembled from Nvidia's public partner disclosures, the two independent Vera Rubin supply-chain teardowns (ksimback/Research-Public and Transurfing Volatility), and his own public comments across videos. Names in the teardowns that he almost certainly already holds in his 100-name index (TSMC, SK Hynix, Micron, Marvell, Nvidia itself, ARM, MediaTek, TSMC-adjacent packaging names) were deliberately excluded from the 25 to match his stated screen.

Inference discipline: no ownership claim is made for any of the 26 names; confidence reflects supply-chain evidence, not his portfolio. H2 2026 (not 2025) is the operative earnings step-up window per the teardowns, one quarter later than his video's phrasing — a good example of why the reconstruction and the report can diverge.
