Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Vera Rubin architecture — Nvidia's next-generation AI GPU platform (successor to Blackwell) that SpaceX committed to build on exclusively; the rack-scale version is codenamed 'Kyber.'
Gigawatt (as a compute unit) — Unit used to describe AI data-center power capacity, roughly equivalent to the power draw of 800,000 homes; per Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's rule of thumb, worth roughly $40-50B of annual AI-infrastructure revenue per gigawatt deployed.
Share lockup unlock — Post-IPO restriction preventing insiders/early investors from selling shares for a set period; SpaceX's first unlock (Aug 6, 2026) freed ~911.5M shares (~$100B), with more tranches through year-end.
ARR (Annual Recurring Revenue) — A revenue run-rate metric SpaceX guided toward ($100B by December 2026) — notably a software/subscription-style metric, reinforcing the 'SpaceX as a compute company' framing.
ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) — Starlink's revenue per subscriber, which fell ~22% YoY even as subscriber count doubled, reflecting expansion into lower-priced international markets.
Capex payback period — How long it takes a capital investment to earn back its own cost; SpaceX's CFO claims its data centers pay back in ~12 months vs. 15-20 years for comparable real-estate-scale infrastructure.
Direct-to-cell — Satellite connectivity delivered straight to unmodified mobile phones (no separate terminal/dish needed), which Starlink is pursuing to compete with carriers like Verizon and AT&T.
