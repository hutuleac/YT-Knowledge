The one-paragraph takeaway

Video Snapshot

  1. David Stout, founder of WebAI (valued at $2.5B, ~200 employees), recounts building an on-device AI company from a $400 loan from his grandfather and one shared computer in a Michigan dorm room.
  2. The conversation covers: the origin of WebAI's local-first AI thesis (2016, pre-transformer era), overcoming investor skepticism that "serious AI can't come from Michigan," the pivot from web services ($750K/yr in college) to deep tech, raising capital after proving the technology works, and a vision for "owning your intelligence" rather than renting it from Big Tech.
  3. Key themes: decentralized AI ownership, edge inference as a constitutional right (Second Amendment analogy), recruiting through relationships not recruiters, faith as a leadership anchor, and the existential risk of centralized data-center AI.
  4. Stout owns ~50% of WebAI; the company is bootstrapped until recently, now raising for a consumer product launch from Austin headquarters.
