Glossary & Entities

Factor volatility — An equal-weighted blend of momentum, value, quality, and beta volatility that Visser tracks separately from index (VIX) volatility; he says it has "broken away" from the index and is now the more reliable signal for hedging AI-heavy portfolios.
200-day moving average (and its slope) — Visser's long-standing rule of thumb for distinguishing a real bull market from a bounce — both price and the average itself need to be trending upward.
Rate of change (ROC) — The percentage move in a price series over a fixed lookback window (Visser uses 50 trading days on his AI thematic portfolio); large positive readings flag crowding/overextension risk rather than an imminent reversal.
Vera Rubin — Nvidia's upcoming next-generation AI GPU architecture, referenced here as the reason Eaton and Schneider Electric (electrical-infrastructure suppliers) are grouped as "participants."
Trueflation — An independent, real-time alternative inflation index Visser prefers over official CPI, which he says has been diverging sharply lower from CPI over the past month.
CDX / CDS — Credit-default-swap-based indices measuring corporate credit risk; used here to show no current credit stress (CDX) even as some hyperscaler CDS trades wider on rising leverage.
Clarity Act — US crypto-market-structure legislation whose odds of 2026 passage fell sharply (per Visser, from near 50% to 17%) over the three weeks before this video.
