The one-paragraph takeaway

Video Snapshot

  1. Jordi Visser argues we're in a short-term AI sentiment pullback ('mini bear market') within a powerful secular bull market driven by structural compute scarcity.
  2. Key thesis: model abundance + Jevons Paradox = exponentially growing compute demand that physical infrastructure cannot match at software speed.
  3. Google's $514B cloud backlog (up from $106B YoY) and 82% cloud growth prove demand outpaces capacity.
  4. China's state-backed AI race (DeepSeek, Moonshot/Kimi) makes this a two-horse geopolitical contest.
  5. Near-term: optical/photonics step-up function (Marvell largest position, added AOI), profit margins at all-time highs, estimate revisions as key signal.
  6. Long-term: bearish on all public AI cos by 2030 unless AGI arrives slower or governments prop markets.
  7. Crypto/blockchain emerging as parallel infrastructure layer as important as AI.
