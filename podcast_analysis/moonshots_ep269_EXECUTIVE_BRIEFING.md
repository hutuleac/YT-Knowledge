#### Key Technical Detail (Alex's 30-second math interlude)

- **Compression prediction**: As we compress more knowledge into weights → **phase transitions in higher-order derivatives** — "follow the compression to the end of the rainbow"

**Timestamp**: 94:00 – 105:00 | **Participants**: All four

- **Case**: US engineer **Stefan Thaylor** filed patent claiming **DABUS** (AI system) as inventor for food container tech
- **Path**: Japan Patent Office → Tokyo District Court → IP High Court → **Supreme Court** (unanimous rejection)
- **Global alignment**: US, UK, EU, Japan all require **natural person** as inventor

#### Subtle Signals
| Signal | Interpretation |
|--------|----------------|
| **Japan as bellwether** | Major patent jurisdiction; ruling closes "forum shopping" for AI inventorship |
| **Thaylor = test case globally** | Same plaintiff (Ryan Abbott's Artificial Inventor Project) litigating worldwide |
| **Food containers = trivial invention** | Deliberate: simple enough that "AI inventorship" is the only novel question |

#### Strategic Implication
