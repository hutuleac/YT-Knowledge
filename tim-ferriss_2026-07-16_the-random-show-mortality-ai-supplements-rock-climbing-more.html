Video Snapshot

Kevin Rose frames the AI model market as consolidating to a 'big three' — Google (Gemini), Anthropic, and OpenAI — plus a wildcard in Elon's xAI/Grok; he notes Anthropic and Google are buying excess compute from 'Colossus' (xAI's data center), implying Grok's own product isn't fully absorbing its capacity.
  3. He predicts the future of AI is 'continuous learning' (self-improving models that get better daily, like a child), with Google betting on a high-throughput/wide-memory architecture and owning the full stack (chips, models, architecture).
  4. He expects AI model access to commoditize into a cheap '~$30/month Netflix-type plan' — i.e., he does NOT see durable pricing power for model labs.
  5. On investing, Rose treats it as a 'scorecard for thinking,' uses Claude Code + APIs for a 20-year angel-investing retrospective, and says he is 'not an investor' in the sense of chasing stock tips; he 'wishes I was an investor in WhisperFlow' (a voice-capture app he uses daily).
  6. Longevity/biotech signal: he and Ferriss funded the University of Washington dog-aging study with rapamycin ('we've got some chips on the table') and name-checked Whole Earth is Medicine and This Too Is Me as favorite companies/projects. No explicit public stock holdings, buys, or ticker symbols are stated.
