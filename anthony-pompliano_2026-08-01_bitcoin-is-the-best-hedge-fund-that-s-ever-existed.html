Video Snapshot

  1. The Leopold hedge fund unwind was a 'cleansing event' - forced liquidation of positions via prime brokers. He was up 400% YTD through June with ~$25B in the fund, suffered ~80% drawdown in July, still has $10B+ remaining. Everyone was crowded into the same AI trades.

  4. Bitcoin is the best hedge fund ever - its early returns mirror Leopold's pattern (huge drawdowns then compounding). The MicroStrategy situation will become the norm as AI creates competitive pressure + debasement.
  5. Over 5 years, $700T global net worth will see money redistributed into crypto (targeting $100T in a decade). Tokenization of real-world assets will unlock dormant capital through fractionalization and daily liquidity.
  6. Borders are breaking down: with crypto + AI + stablecoins, you can run a business from anywhere with AI agents replacing employees. The Philippines economy was already transformed by Axie Infinity.

Names and terms at a glance

Glossary & Entities

Leopold (Hedge Fund) — Unidentified young hedge fund manager (~25yo) that was up 400% YTD through June on ~$25B AUM, suffered ~80% drawdown in July during AI stock unwind, forced to liquidate via prime brokers. Still had $10B+ remaining. Became a cautionary tale about crowded trades + leverage.
Two Forces (Saylor) — Michael Saylor's framework: (1) Competitive innovation destroying businesses, (2) Monetary debasement eroding cash value. Both make Bitcoin a necessary hedge. Every business will face these forces within 5 years.
Molecules vs Bits — Physical constraints (energy, chips, labor - molecules) limit digital transformation (AI, software, algorithms - bits). AI development is gated by physical infrastructure buildout, not algorithmic breakthroughs alone.
Speed Crash — Rapid market corrections enabled by faster AI-driven decision-making. What took 30 years now takes 3 years. More frequent as leverage decreases and AI agents enter decision-making.
Tokenization — Digitization of real-world assets (real estate, stocks, bonds) onto blockchain, enabling fractional ownership, 24/7 trading, and daily liquidity. Jordi sees $700T global net worth redistributing via $100T crypto within a decade.
Agentic Trading — AI agents making autonomous financial decisions, including capital rotation, tax loss harvesting, and cross-asset allocation. Platforms like Or Public and Figure Markets bring this to retail investors.
Being Great vs Achieving Greatness — Distinction between external accomplishments (what you build/hold) and internal character (how you respond to life's worst moments). Ben Wilson embodies 'being great' during his cancer battle.
Clean Sheet — Hedge fund term for starting over with a fresh portfolio after a major drawdown. Jordi suggests Leopold's situation was a cleansing event that forced de-risking and re-approach to positions.
